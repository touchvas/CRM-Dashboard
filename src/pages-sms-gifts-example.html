Campaign Tools
Send SMS & Award Prizes
Pro Tip: Use the tools below to send mass SMS campaigns or award gifts to your users. Both features support template selection and custom messaging.
Send Mass SMS

Send personalized SMS to your users with pre-built templates or custom messages. Include variables like name, amount, currency, and dates.

Award Gifts

Reward your users with cash (withdrawable or non-withdrawable) or special bonuses like cashback, premium features, or VIP status.

SMS Features
  • 2 Pre-built Templates - Welcome bonus and promotional offers
  • Variable Insertion - Insert {CUSTOMER_NAME}, {AMOUNT}, {CURRENCY}, {DATE}
  • Character Counter - See message and SMS count in real-time
  • Live Preview - Preview formatted message before sending
  • Recipient Selection - Target specific user groups
Gifts Features
  • 2 Gift Types - Cash (with withdrawal options) or Bonuses
  • Cash Options - Withdrawable or non-withdrawable
  • 7 Bonus Types - Cashback, Premium features, VIP status, and more
  • Summary Preview - See exactly what will be awarded
  • Flexible Amount - Set any amount for cash rewards
